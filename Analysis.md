### 📉 Behavior of Wallets in Lower Score Ranges (e.g., 0–200)
Wallets in this range are marked by risky, bot-like, or exploitative behavior.

###🔎 Key Characteristics (Hypothesized)
- High liquidation_count and liquidation_volume_usd
 → Frequent or large-value liquidations suggest poor debt management.
- low repay_ratio
 → Indicates borrowing without repayment.
- Low unique_days_active and time_span_days
 → Wallets show short-lived, bursty activity—likely bots or flash loan actors.
- High transactions_per_day
 → Rapid, automated transaction behavior over a short span.
- Dominance of borrow and liquidationCall actions
 → Reflects high leverage usage without long-term engagement.

### 🧠 Inferred Behavior
- These wallets likely:
- Engage in high-risk speculation
- Attempt arbitrage or flash loan strategies
- Exhibit short-term, unsustainable DeFi usage
- Pose greater risk to the protocol

### 📈 Behavior of Wallets in Higher Score Ranges (e.g., 800–1000)
Wallets in this range show responsible and consistent DeFi usage.

### 🔎 Key Characteristics (Hypothesized)
Zero or very low liquidation_count / volume_usd
→ Indicates effective risk control.
High repay_ratio (≥1)
→ Suggests full or excess repayment behavior.
High unique_days_active and time_span_days
→ Consistent, long-term usage of the protocol.
Balanced deposit and repay activity
→ Indicates planned borrowing and repayment.
Lower transactions_per_day
→ More human-like, non-bursty interaction pattern.

### 🧠 Inferred Behavior
These wallets:
Are power users or long-term participants
Contribute to platform stability and liquidity
Display financial prudence and discipline
Are ideal candidates for DeFi credit/lending programs

### ⚠️ Insights and Limitations
Heuristic Dependency
The scoring system is based on a heuristic proxy. While informative, it simplifies complex behavior.

#### Feature Importance
Analyzing the LightGBM model's feature importances can highlight key predictive behaviors.
Limited Scope
Only Aave V2 data is used. A comprehensive credit profile could benefit from:
Other DeFi protocols
Centralized exchange (CEX) data
Off-chain financial history
DeFi's Dynamic Nature
Wallet behavior changes rapidly. This static score might not reflect the most up-to-date risk profile.
Future work could include dynamic or time-weighted scoring.

### 🧠 Conclusion
- This analysis offers a foundational look at credit behavior in the Aave V2 ecosystem.
- By clustering and analyzing score segments, we gain insights into user behavior, financial reliability, and protocol risk.
- Further analysis on transaction histories, feature importance, or cluster-based behavior groups can enhance this framework.
